The workflow

From a signal in a noisy club to a statement a creator can trust

Four stages turn raw monitored audio into attributable usage. Every stage is designed for intermittent connectivity, DJ manipulation, fragmented rights, and human review.

01

Audio monitoring

FFmpeg listeners on agreed FM and web streams, plus Raspberry Pi micro-listeners in consented clubs, bars, hotels and taxi routes, capture 30-second mono chunks.

Offline-first: an encrypted local spool survives outages and retries on a unique chunk ID. Hashes and manifests travel first; raw audio only under policy.

02

Robust fingerprinting

Spectral peaks become landmark pairs hashed on relative log-frequency ratios — so pitch shifts and beat-matched mixes still match. A tempo-aware vote across 0.90–1.10 timing scales proposes candidate windows.

Every candidate records schema ID, matcher version, votes and confidence — explainable evidence, never a black-box verdict.

03

Rights splits

Recordings (ISRC) and compositions (ISWC) carry versioned split sheets for master, composition, performance and mechanical rights — producers, artists, labels, writers and publishers included.

Draft → active → superseded. A database guard rejects any active sheet that doesn’t total exactly 10,000 basis points.

04

Statements & payout

Verified plays meet the tariff and source weight in force at the time of use. Deterministic Decimal arithmetic allocates whole UGX by largest remainder, so recipient totals always equal gross.

Payouts are idempotent outbox instructions to approved wallet rails — enabled only after CMO governance and telecom sandbox certification. Disputed amounts are held, not redistributed.

A fingerprint match is evidence, not a payable event. Human or approved-policy review, dispute status, an active split, a governed tariff version and finance approval all stand between a candidate detection and any money movement.

Trust & safety

Privacy and financial controls are design constraints, not add-ons

Audio minimization

Fingerprints and signed manifests are the default retention unit. Encrypted clips need a defined purpose, access log and deletion schedule.

Wallet privacy

Only ciphertext, an HMAC for duplicate detection and a key reference are stored. Full wallet numbers and tokens never touch code or logs.

Financial safety gates

Candidates, disputes, inactive splits, unverified accounts and unapproved tariffs cannot enter a payout batch.

Audit everything

Append-only events for catalog edits, split activation, reviews, tariff changes, calculation runs and provider callbacks.

Visible venue notices

Pilot locations carry a listener notice and contact path. No covert microphone deployment — ever.

Bias monitoring

Recall is measured for regional languages, older recordings, female creators and emerging artists — not just studio masters.
